Vault windows

Each room gets a window on its back wall looking out on a scene: ocean, space, a city at night, mountains… The scene is picked at random per room (and stays the same for that room), sits behind the furniture and moves slowly. All art is drawn in code, no photos.

1 · Window frames

Three frames to pick from. The frame could also be random per room.

2 · Scenes (10)

Every scene is an SVG in a 120×72 box that fills whatever frame it's in. Movement is slow and small so it doesn't pull your eye away from the robots.

3 · In a room

Window on the back wall, behind the furniture and robots, with a soft spill of its light on the wall. Click "Shuffle room scenes" to see other combinations.

4 · Behaviour (proposal)

WhatHow
