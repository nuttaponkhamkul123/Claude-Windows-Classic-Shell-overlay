Vault room shapes

The Vault becomes a dig site: a grid 4 blocks wide, as tall as needed. Each workspace's room is a set of connected blocks dug out of the rock — a bar (today's look), a box, an L, a reversed L, a T… Normal keeps every room a full-width rectangle; Free lets you shape and place them. Try it: switch to Free, turn on Build mode, pick a shape, drag rooms, R to rotate, click rock next to the selected room to dig, right-click a block to fill it back in.

How it would work in the app

WhatHow
Grid4 columns × as many rows as needed. One block = ¼ of the HUD width × one room height (about 116 × 88 px): room for 2 robots and 1 piece of furniture.
Normal / FreeA switch in Build mode and in Settings. Normal: full-width rooms, stacked (today). Free: each room keeps its shape and spot. Your Free layout is saved even while you're in Normal.
ShapesBar 4, bar 2, block, box, L, reversed L, T, step — or paint any connected shape block by block.
RulesRooms can't overlap and must stay in one piece. Red ghost = not allowed. Removing a block that would split the room is refused.
Inside a roomNo wall between a room's own blocks. Blocks side by side share one floor; stacked blocks are two floors joined by a ladder through a hatch. Robots walk each floor and climb the ladders.
Robots per roomAbout 2 per block; the rest go to the drawer under the room, as now.
New workspaceGets a bar-2 room in the first free spot (Free mode).
Furniture / windowEach block can hold furniture; the existing Builder mode moves pieces between a room's blocks; the window goes on any block's back wall.
