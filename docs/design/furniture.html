Vault furniture

Every task piece gets a second look (same job, same moving parts), so rooms in the Vault don't all look alike. Seven new decor pieces give idle robots more to do. Pieces are drawn in a 64×56 box; this sheet shows them at 2× size.
Off = how a piece looks when nobody's using it (dimmed, still)

1 · Task furniture — original and new look

A robot walks to the piece for its current step. Each room picks one look per piece (from the room's name), so a room keeps its look; pieces that pop in for a task pick one at random.

2 · Decor — for idle robots

No task uses these. Idle robots wander over and use them; the piece comes alive while they're there. Each room gets one decor piece.

3 · Sample rooms

Three task pieces plus one decor piece per room, in the room's own looks.
